# Synthetic sample: EV adoption in Europe (demo only)

Battery electric vehicles accounted for 22% of new car registrations across the European sample markets in 2024.
Registrations grew by 31% year over year in the Nordic sample markets, the fastest growth in the European sample.
Western European sample markets reported higher average vehicle prices than Southern European markets in the same period.
Adoption in Southern European sample markets stayed below 12% of new registrations, reflecting lower household incomes in the sample.
Fleet purchases by rental and logistics companies made up 18% of electric registrations in the European sample.
Analysts in the sample expect battery electric vehicles to reach about 35% of European registrations by 2030 if current policies continue.
