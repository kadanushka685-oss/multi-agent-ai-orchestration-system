# Synthetic sample: EV adoption in Asia (demo only)

Battery electric vehicles accounted for 41% of new car registrations across the Asia sample markets in 2024.
China-based manufacturers in the Asia sample held roughly 60% of battery electric vehicle volume.
Registrations in Southeast Asian sample markets grew by 48% year over year, starting from a much smaller base.
Two-wheeler electrification in the Asia sample reached 9% of new sales, and it is tracked separately from cars.
Urban adoption in the Asia sample is driven by ride-hailing fleets and shared mobility programmes.
Government procurement rules in several Asia sample markets required 25% of public transit fleets to be electric by 2025.
