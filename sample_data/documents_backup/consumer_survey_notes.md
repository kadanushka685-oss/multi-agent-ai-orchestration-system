# Synthetic sample: consumer survey notes (demo only)

In the synthetic consumer survey, 58% of respondents in Europe said range anxiety was their main concern about electric cars.
Battery electric vehicles accounted for 18% of new car registrations across European markets in the survey period.
Respondents in the Asia sample ranked charging convenience as their top purchase factor, ahead of price.
About 46% of survey respondents said they would consider an electric car as their next purchase.
Respondents under 35 were twice as likely as older respondents to report interest in shared mobility.
Concern about battery degradation was cited by 27% of respondents in the survey.
