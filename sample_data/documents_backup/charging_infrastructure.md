# Synthetic sample: charging infrastructure (demo only)

The European sample network had 1.2 public chargers per 100 kilometres of major road in 2024.
The Asia sample reported 3.5 public chargers per 100 kilometres, reflecting denser urban deployment.
Fast chargers above 150 kW made up 14% of public charging points in the European sample.
Charging speed and reliability were the most cited barriers in the sample consumer complaints in both regions.
Utilities in the Asia sample co-invested in about 40% of new charging hubs.
Residents of apartments in the European sample reported difficulty installing home chargers in 55% of surveyed buildings.
