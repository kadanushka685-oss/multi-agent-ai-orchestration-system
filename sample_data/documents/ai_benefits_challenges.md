# Benefits and Challenges of Artificial Intelligence (Synthetic Demo Data)

AI can improve productivity by automating repetitive and time-consuming tasks.
Machine learning can help organisations identify patterns and make data-driven decisions.
AI-powered recommendation systems can improve user experiences through personalised suggestions.
AI systems may produce inaccurate outputs when trained on incomplete or poor-quality data.
Biased training datasets can lead to unfair predictions and discriminatory outcomes.
Data privacy, security, transparency and accountability remain important challenges in AI adoption.
