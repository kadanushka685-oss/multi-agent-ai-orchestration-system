# AI Industry Trends (Synthetic Demo Data)

Generative AI is increasingly used for content creation, coding assistance and business productivity.
Small language models can support AI applications with lower computational requirements.
Retrieval-Augmented Generation combines language models with external knowledge sources to improve responses.
Edge AI processes data locally on devices to reduce latency and dependence on cloud connectivity.
AI automation is being adopted for customer support, document processing and workflow management.
Organisations increasingly focus on AI governance, model evaluation and responsible deployment.
